Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

TimeTracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

What is stored

Your time entries, settings and corrections are stored locally on your device, in a plain-text file (taps.log) inside the app's Documents directory.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No network requests.
  • No access to your employer's systems, badge terminal, calendar, contacts, health data, or location.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup for your device, iOS may include the app's data in your own encrypted backup. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by this app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its data. Use Settings → Export taps.log to keep a copy.
