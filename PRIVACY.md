# Privacy Policy

TimeTracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

- All time entries, settings and corrections are stored locally on your device,
  in a plain-text file (`taps.log`) inside the app's Documents directory.
- The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network
  requests.
- The app does not access your employer's systems, badge terminal, calendar,
  contacts, health data, or location.
- If you use iCloud Backup for your device, iOS may include the app's data in
  your own encrypted backup. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by
  this app.
- Deleting the app deletes its data. Use **Settings → Export taps.log** to keep
  a copy.

Contact: [open an issue](https://github.com/dovidio/time-tracking/issues).

Last updated: 22 October 2026.
