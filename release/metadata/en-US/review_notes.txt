TimeTrackete is a local, single-user time ledger. No accounts, no login, no account deletion, no user-generated content, no paid content, no network access.

Purpose: for employees whose badge terminal records time but does not show a balance. It is a personal ledger, not a connection to any employer system or hardware.

Setup: no credentials or sample files. Open the app; Settings sets weekly hours, working days, optional standard lunch, rounding and holidays. The large button records stamps (1 in, 2 out, 3 back, 4 out); the pencil edits a day; History shows balances.
